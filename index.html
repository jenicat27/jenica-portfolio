    02Research

    My research explored common barriers to recycling, the audience most likely to engage with sustainability-focused products, and opportunities to encourage more sustainable habits.

    Recycling barriers

    ~40%of Californians recycle properly, according to research I reviewed

    Many people face challenges when trying to recycle correctly:

    • Uncertainty about which materials are recyclable
    • Difficulty locating nearby recycling centers

    This highlighted a potential opportunity to make recycling information more accessible and actionable.

    Target audience

    18–35younger adults, identified as a potential audience

    Understanding their interests and habits could help inform an engaging, accessible approach to encouraging sustainable behavior in a mobile experience.

    Product opportunities

    • Gamification
    • Personalized goals
    • Rewards

    Ways to encourage participation and build consistent recycling habits. Potential monetization strategies could also be investigated to support the product's long-term sustainability.

    03Ideation

    EcoLoop aims to make sustainable living more accessible by helping people make eco-friendly decisions in their everyday lives while fostering a connected, environmentally conscious community.

    The core idea was to integrate AI-powered guidance into daily routines and connect individual actions to collective neighborhood impact. By making sustainable choices easier to understand and act on, EcoLoop could encourage consistent habits and create a positive feedback loop between individuals and their communities.

    Core features

    1. 01

      Scan & Identify

      Use AI-powered computer vision to identify items and provide guidance on how to recycle or dispose of them properly.

      Responds to: not knowing which materials are recyclable

    2. 02

      Location-Aware Guidance

      Help users find nearby recycling centers, specialized drop-off locations, and eco-friendly stores through location-based recommendations.

      Responds to: difficulty locating recycling centers

    3. 03

      Proactive Nudges

      Offer timely, location-aware reminders about sustainable actions users can take nearby, such as an upcoming electronics recycling drop-off.

      Responds to: building consistent habits

    4. 04

      Track Your Impact

      Provide a personalized dashboard where users can monitor their sustainability efforts, track estimated environmental impact, and earn rewards for participating in eco-friendly activities.

      Responds to: gamification, personalized goals, rewards

    5. 05

      Community & Collective Action

      Connect users with local sustainability initiatives and neighborhood activities, turning individual eco-friendly choices into opportunities for collective action.

      Responds to: engaging younger adults together

    These are proposed features of the EcoLoop concept, not a launched product.

    04Design

    Using Google Stitch, we designed EcoLoop's UX/UI flow with a focus on intuitive navigation and accessibility. Our goal was to make sustainable living easier by helping users identify recyclable items, locate nearby recycling resources, track their progress, and connect with an eco-conscious community.

    We designed a scan feature that allows users to take a picture of an item they're unsure how to recycle. EcoLoop uses AI-powered image recognition to identify the item and provide guidance on whether it is recyclable and how it should be disposed of. This feature aims to reduce confusion and make responsible disposal easier in everyday situations.

    Project · Product Design × Social Experiences

    Real Quest

    A new way to make everyday moments more interactive.

    BeReal Product Management Externship

    Real Quest is a proposed social feature concept for BeReal that gives friends a new way to connect through weekly guesses, shared experiences, and authentic everyday moments.

    • Product research
    • Competitor benchmarking
    • Ideation
    • User flow & UI prototype
    • Participant feedback
    • Presented to BeReal's CEO

    01The Problem

    Driving engagement without losing authenticity.

    BeReal encourages users to share authentic, unfiltered moments of their everyday lives. The challenge was to explore how the platform could increase user engagement and monthly active users (MAUs) while staying true to its mission of genuine social connection.

    Grow

    Increase engagement and monthly active users (MAUs)

    &

    Protect

    Stay true to BeReal's mission of genuine social connection

    Rather than relying on traditional engagement tactics, I explored opportunities to make the BeReal experience more interactive and give users new reasons to return, connect, and participate.

    02Research

    I explored how BeReal encourages daily participation and compared its features with Snapchat to identify opportunities to increase engagement while preserving authentic social connection.

    BeReal · building daily habits

    • Daily notificationEncourages users to capture spontaneous moments within the same two-minute window.
    • Friends of FriendsHelps users discover connections and engage with their social circles.

    These mechanics highlight the value of shared moments and daily participation.

    vs

    Competitor benchmarking · Snapchat

    • SnapstreaksEncourage consistent interaction between friends through ongoing daily exchanges.
    • Stories & SpotlightGive users fresh content to discover and share.
    • Snap MapCreates opportunities for location-based discovery and social connection.

    Recurring interactions, content discovery, and shared experiences can create additional reasons for users to open an app.

    Opportunity

    Build on BeReal's spontaneous, friend-centered experience by introducing more interactive ways for users to participate together. Rather than relying solely on daily posting, a new feature could give friends another reason to connect and return to the app.

    03Ideation

    Where it started

    Weekly prompts

    Prompts shared between friends. At the end of each week, users would reveal their assigned prompts and match them with a BeReal they had captured during the week.

    A heavily gamified approach risked distracting from BeReal's emphasis on authentic, everyday moments. Sharing shouldn't feel like a competition or obligation.

    Where it landed

    From prompts to guessing

    A guessing-based social experience designed to help friends learn more about one another, centered on curiosity, personal discovery, and meaningful interactions between friends.

    The core idea

    Real Quest gives friends a new way to engage with each other through playful guessing, adding an interactive layer to BeReal while keeping the focus on genuine social connection.

    Design goal

    Create a feature that gives users another reason to connect and interact, while preserving the spontaneity and authenticity that make BeReal unique.

    04Design

    I translated the Real Quest concept into a user flow and UI prototype designed to make connecting with friends feel natural, interactive, and true to BeReal's emphasis on authenticity.

    Design goal

    The experience was designed to introduce a new way for friends to interact while keeping BeReal's everyday posts at the center. By combining hidden predictions, weekly reveals, and familiar social interactions, Real Quest turns ordinary moments into opportunities for connection.

    05Outcome

    I developed a prototype of Real Quest and presented the concept to BeReal CEO Ben Moore during an externship meeting. The feedback highlighted the potential for Real Quest to strengthen BeReal's focus on close friendships and authentic social connections.

    Feedback from BeReal

    A promising direction for the product team to explore, with a focus on the relationships between close friends on BeReal.

    Summary of feedback from Ben Moore, CEO of BeReal. He particularly appreciated how Real Quest connected to the platform's identity as a space for genuine social sharing.

    User feedback · focus group survey

    To evaluate the concept, I surveyed 8 participants across different usage levels. It was a small, exploratory sample, not representative of all BeReal users.

    8participants 4 frequent users · 2 occasional users · 2 non-users
